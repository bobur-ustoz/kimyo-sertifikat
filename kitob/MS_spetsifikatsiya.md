## Fan mazmuni (baholanadigan elementlar)
**Umumiy kimyo (10 mavzu):** 1 asosiy tushunchalar (atom, molekula, allotropiya, Ar/Mr, mol, valentlik); 2 qonunlar (massa saqlanish, tarkib doimiyligi, gaz qonunlari, Avogadro, ekvivalent); 3 davriy sistema, atom tuzilishi, izotop/izobar/izoton/izoelektron, elektron konfiguratsiya (Pauli, Klechkovskiy, Gund), kvant sonlari, yadro reaksiyalari; 4 kimyoviy bog'lanish turlari, struktura, kristall panjara, gibridlanish; 5 reaksiya tezligi; 6 muvozanat, Le-Shatelye; 7 eritmalar (foiz, molar, molyal, normal, oleum, eruvchanlik, titr); 8 dissotsilanish, α, ion almashinish, pH/pOH, gidroliz; 9 oksidlanish darajasi, OQR va tenglash usullari; 10 kuchlanishlar qatori, elektroliz (eritma/suyuqlanma), Faradey.
**Anorganik (5):** 1 sinflar va genetik bog'lanish, reaksiya turlari; 2 oksid, asos, kislota, tuz; 3 IA metallar; 4 IIA, IIIA, d-metallar, suv qattiqligi; 5 metallmaslar (IVA–VIIA), vodorod, nodir gazlar, mineral o'g'itlar.
**Organik (10):** 1 klassifikatsiya, tuzilish nazariyasi, izomeriya, nomenklatura, reaksiya turlari, alkan, sikloalkan; 2 alken, alkadiyen, alkin; 3 arenlar, neft, tabiiy gaz, toshko'mir; 4 bir atomli spirt, ko'p atomli spirt, fenol, aromatik spirt; 5 aldegid va keton; 6 karbon kislotalar; 7 oddiy va murakkab efir, sovun, yog'; 8 uglevodlar; 9 nitrobirikma, amin, aminokislota, oqsil; 10 YuMB (polimerlar, kauchuk, tola).
**Kimyoviy tahlil (2):** 1 laboratoriya xavfsizligi, jihozlar, aralashmalarni ajratish (tindirish, filtrlash, bug'latish, magnitlash, distillash), eritma tayyorlash; 2 anorganik/organik birikmalarni olish, sifat reaksiyalar, tajribalar.
Kognitiv daraja: quyi (bilish, yangi vaziyatda qo'llash, sodda masala, grafik/jadval) va yuqori (murakkab masala, grafik/jadval tahlili, jihozlardan xavfsiz foydalanish).

## Yozma ish baholash (M — yechish usuli, A — arifmetik hisob)
- **41 (umumiy), 3–5 band, 25 ball:** jami M 13–15 ball, A 10–12 ball. 3 bandli: 1-band M 7–10 + A 3–5; 2-band M 2–4 + A 1–3; 3-band M 2–5 + A 2–4. 4 bandli: 1-band M 6–8 + A 3–5; 2-band M 2–4 + A 1–3; 3-band M 2–5 + A 2–4; 4-band M 2–4 + A 1–3. 5 bandli: 1-band M 6–8 + A 3–5; 2-band M 1–4 + A 1–3; 3-band M 2–5 + A 2–4; 4-band M 1–4 + A 1–3; 5-band M 2–5 + A 1–2.
- **42 (organik), 25 ball, FAQAT M (hisob-kitob A yo'q!):** 3 bandli: 11–13 / 9–10 / 3–5; 4 bandli: 11–13 / 9–10 / 2–3 / 2–3; 7 bandli: har bandga 3–5 ball. Demak 42-topshiriq hisoblashsiz — reaksiya/zanjir/aniqlash turidagi topshiriq (3, 4 yoki 7 band).
- **43 anorganik:** 41 bilan bir xil jadval (3–5 band, M 13–15 + A 10–12). **43 kimyoviy tahlil:** 3 bandli – 1-band M 10–13 + A 2–3, 2-band M 4–6, 3-band M 2–4 (jami M 22–23, A 2–3); 4 bandli – M 23–25, A 1–2; 5 bandli – M 23–25, A 1–2.
